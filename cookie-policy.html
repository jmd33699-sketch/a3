1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small alphanumeric files placed on your browser or device when you navigate our digital platforms. They allow our website to remember your preferences, secure our allocation forms against malicious automated traffic, and maintain session state across our collection pages.

2. Categories of Cookies We Employ

We classify our cookies strictly based on operational necessity:

  • Strictly Necessary Cookies: Essential for browsing our timepiece catalog, rendering navigation drawers, and securing our allocation submission forms against cross-site request forgery (CSRF). These cookies do not require prior consent.
  • Analytical & Performance Cookies: We utilize Google Analytics 4 (Measurement ID: G-0LY0HY7L01) with IP anonymization enabled. These cookies collect aggregated data regarding which master timepieces generate the greatest collector interest and average visit duration, allowing us to refine our horological presentation.
  • Preference & Functionality Cookies: Store UI settings such as dismissed alerts, active search queries, or accordion interaction states.

3. Third-Party Advertising Pixels

Mechanical Hour rejects intrusive third-party behavioral advertising pixels. We do not sell your browsing history to advertising brokers or engage in cross-site retargeting campaigns.

4. Managing and Disabling Cookies

You may adjust your browser preferences at any time to block or delete cookies. Disabling strictly necessary cookies may impair certain features such as the search modal or allocation form. Configuration instructions for major browsers:

  • Apple Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Prevent cross-site tracking / Block all cookies.
  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data.
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Enhanced Tracking Protection.
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Manage and delete cookies.

5. Inquiries Regarding Cookies

For any questions regarding our tracking technologies or data policies, contact our privacy concierge at contact@mechanicalhour.com.
