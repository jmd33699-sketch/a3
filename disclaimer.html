1. Nature of Mechanical Chronometry

A mechanical watch is an intricate micro-kinetic engine powered exclusively by an unwinding coiled spring and regulated by an oscillating balance wheel. Unlike battery-powered quartz oscillators or satellite-synchronized atomic clocks, a mechanical balance is subject to physical environmental variables including gravitational position, ambient temperature shifts, and kinetic wrist motion. Nominal daily rate tolerance for our regulated calibres is ±1 to ±3 seconds per day under standard wearing conditions.

2. Water Resistance & Gasket Integrity

Water resistance ratings (e.g., 30m, 100m, 300m) are determined under static laboratory pressure tests according to ISO 22810 / ISO 6425. Water resistance is not a permanent attribute; rubber sealing gaskets age naturally and can degrade through exposure to chemical solvents, extreme heat, or chlorinated swimming pools. We recommend having your timepiece's water resistance verified annually by our Geneva service department.

3. Digital Imagery & Metal Tone Rendering

While our studio macro photography is executed under calibrated daylight illumination, precious metal alloys (such as 18K 5N rose gold, white gold, and platinum 950) react dynamically to ambient light. Digital displays with differing color gamuts (sRGB, DCI-P3, Apple TrueTone) may exhibit slight chromatic shifts compared to physical inspection at our salon.

4. Independence & Non-Affiliation

MECHANICAL HOUR is an entirely independent Swiss horological manufacture. References to historical horologists, classical complications (such as Breguet overcoils or Tourbillons), or technical standards are strictly historical and academic. Mechanical Hour is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by any multinational luxury conglomerate.

5. Horological Advice Disclaimer

The technical monographs and maintenance treatises published in our chronicles are provided for educational and connoisseur appreciation. Patrons should never attempt to open casebacks, adjust hairsprings, or apply lubricants themselves. All service must be conducted by certified watchmakers to preserve the perpetual guarantee.
