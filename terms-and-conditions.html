1. Scope & Identification of the House

MECHANICAL HOUR SA is an independent Swiss corporation (Société Anonyme) incorporated under the laws of the Canton of Geneva, Switzerland, with registered salons and ateliers at 12 Rue du Rhône, 1204 Genève. We specialize in the development, bench manufacture, and restoration of high-complication mechanical wristwatches.

2. Timepiece Allocations & Bespoke Commissions

Due to the rigorous hand finishing required for each movement, annual production is strictly limited:

  • Manufacture Edition Allocations: Numbered catalog models. An allocation is confirmed upon receipt of a 50% reservation deposit, with the remaining balance due upon completion and chronometer certification prior to armored delivery.
  • Piece Unique Commissions: Custom movement engineering, astronomical complications, or personalized cases. Commissions require an initial non-refundable engineering deposit to cover CAD simulations and custom tooling.

3. Handcrafted Tolerances & Artisanal Character

Every bridge, bevel, dial, and hand is shaped and polished by individual human artisans. Subtle variations in anglage reflection, hand-guilloché engine turning, and grand feu enamel kiln crystallization are natural marks of authenticity that distinguish human watchmaking from mass industrial production.

4. Intellectual Property

All calibre architectures, gear train patents, aesthetic case silhouettes, photographs, technical monographs, and the MECHANICAL HOUR trademark are the exclusive intellectual property of MECHANICAL HOUR SA. Unauthorized reproduction or reverse engineering is strictly prohibited under Swiss and international law.

5. Delivery, Title & Risk of Transit

All timepieces are transported via specialized armored couriers (e.g., Ferrari Group, Malca-Amit) with 100% full declared value transit insurance. Risk of loss passes to the patron upon physical transfer and signed handover.

6. Perpetual Warranty & Service Exclusions

Every Mechanical Hour watch carries our Perpetual Guarantee against defects in movement materials and assembly. The warranty does not cover damage resulting from catastrophic external impact, unauthorized third-party disassembly, or immersion beyond rated atmospheric pressure tolerances.

7. Applicable Law & Arbitration

These terms are governed exclusively by the substantive laws of Switzerland. Any disputes arising from or in connection with timepiece sales shall be submitted to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Ordinary Courts of the Canton of Geneva, with the right of appeal to the Swiss Federal Supreme Court (Tribunal Fédéral) in Lausanne.
